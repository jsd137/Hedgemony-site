Common questions

How do I get my existing clients in?

Settings → Import clients. You can pick people from Contacts, or import a CSV spreadsheet exported from Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets. The import shows you a preview of what will be created and any problems it found before anything is saved. Only a name is required, and column order doesn't matter.

Is my data backed up?

Your work is saved on your device the instant you tap it, signal or not. If your device is signed into iCloud, Hedgemony also backs up to your private iCloud automatically. Settings shows you plainly whether backup is on.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Settings → Prepare export gives you plain CSV files of your clients, visits, and money. The client file imports straight back into a fresh install, so your business is yours to take anywhere.

What happens when a client changes from weekly to biweekly?

Edit their plan and the schedule reflows automatically. You'll see exactly how many upcoming visits will move before you confirm, and completed history is never touched.

Rained out?

Open the day in the Schedule tab and use "Move all" to push every remaining stop to another day. Everyone keeps their place in the rotation, so a biweekly client stays biweekly.
